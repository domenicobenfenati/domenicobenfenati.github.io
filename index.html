About

Naples

I am a research fellow at DIETI, University of Naples Federico II, in the IKNOS Lab, and I work with the PICUS Lab in the same department.

My PhD, in the Italian National Programme in Artificial Intelligence (Agrifood and Environment track), is on data quality: how to measure what is missing or wrong in a dataset, and how to repair it. The fellowship ended in October 2026; I defend the thesis in 2027.

Approach

Satellite tiles with gaps, farm records with missing fields, web pages with the wrong label: before a model trains on them, I find what is wrong in the data and repair it.
