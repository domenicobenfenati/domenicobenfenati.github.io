About

Naples

I am a research fellow at DIETI, University of Naples Federico II, in the IKNOS Lab, and I work with the PICUS Lab in the same department.

I took my MSc in Computer Science Engineering at Federico II in 2023, with a thesis on populating knowledge graphs with generative models. I then joined the Italian National PhD Programme in Artificial Intelligence, in its Agrifood and Environment track. My thesis is on data quality: how to measure what is missing or wrong in a dataset, and how to repair it.

Lately that means Sentinel-2 time series, where clouds hide part of almost every acquisition. I reconstruct the covered pixels, and I built EarthJev, which reads all 12 bands of a patch and answers questions with a typed value. I also work on knowledge graphs and retrieval-augmented generation for agriculture and nutrigenetics, and on attacks against image retrieval.

Research

More on each line

  1. Data quality and data-centric AI

    Measuring and repairing training data: missing values, noisy labels, gaps in time series. With colleagues I mapped how the agrifood literature handles it.

    Comput. Electron. Agric. 2025KES 2026

  2. Satellite image time series

    A probabilistic model that reconstructs cloud-covered pixels across a Sentinel-2 series, and EarthJev, which answers typed questions from the 12 bands of a patch.

    arXiv 2611.02096KES 2026

  3. Knowledge graphs and RAG

    Generative models that populate multimedia knowledge graphs, and retrieval that keeps answers tied to an ontology, for agriculture and nutrigenetics.

    IEEE TAI 2026Neural Comput. Appl. 2026IEEE SMC 2025KES 2024

  4. Retrieval under attack

    Focused crawlers and multimodal web page classification; how embedding-space attacks degrade retrieval with pathology foundation models.

    ACM 2026IC3K 2024MEDES 2023
