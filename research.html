Research

I work on the quality of the data that AI models learn from, mostly in Earth observation and agrifood. Four lines run in parallel; each lists the papers behind it.

  1. Data quality and data-centric AI

    Measuring and repairing training data: missing values, noisy labels, gaps in time series. With colleagues I mapped how the agrifood literature handles it.

    Comput. Electron. Agric. 2025KES 2026

  2. Satellite image time series

    A probabilistic model that reconstructs cloud-covered pixels across a Sentinel-2 series, and EarthJev, which answers typed questions from the 12 bands of a patch.

    arXiv 2611.02096KES 2026

  3. Knowledge graphs and RAG

    Generative models that populate multimedia knowledge graphs, and retrieval that keeps answers tied to an ontology, for agriculture and nutrigenetics.

    IEEE TAI 2026Neural Comput. Appl. 2026IEEE SMC 2025KES 2024

  4. Retrieval under attack

    Focused crawlers and multimodal web page classification; how embedding-space attacks degrade retrieval with pathology foundation models.

    CIKM 2026IC3K 2024MEDES 2023

EarthJev model · Nov 2026

EarthJev reads a Sentinel-2 L2A patch in all 12 bands and answers each question with a typed value instead of free text.

Written with Antonio Maria Rinaldi and Cristian Tommasino. Evaluated on CORINE land cover and RSVQAxBEN.

Preprint, arXiv 2611.02096 Code on GitHub Weights on Hugging Face
